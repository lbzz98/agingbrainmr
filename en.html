AgingBrain MR

Structured brain MRI assessment in patients over 60

Guides the radiologist through the reading of brain aging: atrophy, white matter, perivascular spaces, lacunes, microbleeds and other hemorrhagic lesions, with age-adjusted judgment and a concise report text.

What it assesses

  • Atrophy: MTA, GCA and Koedam visual scales, or the percentiles of a volumetric report for each side.
  • White matter: deep and periventricular Fazekas, multispot pattern.
  • Perivascular spaces (Potter), lacunes, microbleeds (MARS) and other hemorrhagic lesions.
  • Cerebral amyloid angiopathy according to the Boston criteria 2.0, and the total SVD score.

Disclaimer

AgingBrain MR is a reporting-support and training tool for radiologists. It is not a medical device, does not replace the radiologist's judgment and must not be used as the sole basis for diagnostic or therapeutic decisions.

Languages

The app follows the phone's language (Italian or English). You can choose a different one just for this app in Settings → AgingBrain MR → Language.
